Why this shape. The question isn't "what did I spend" — /spend already answers that. It's "did my change help, and what did it cost elsewhere?" A shared x-axis with one annotation rail makes the tradeoff visible: on 09-09 the model swap cut tokens per run by a third, and the failure rate climbed with it. Two rows apart, same vertical line. That pairing is invisible on any single-metric chart.
